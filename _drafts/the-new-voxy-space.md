Well, I've finally done it, I updated my website! Welcome to the new voxy.space! 🎉

I haven't done much with this place in the last couple of years, but in these current times, it felt more and more important to ensure this was a little corner of Internet where I can reliably share the stuff I'm doing, without requiring readers to trudge through swathes of inauthentic AI slop to access it. So, I got to work, and cooked up this new version of my site, with greater focus on my projects, and hopefully more blogposts to come!

## An identity crisis

This redesign was a long time in the making, partly because for a while, I couldn't really decide what I wanted my "branding" to look like. Since launching the previous version of my website, I changed my logo multiple times, mostly in a pretty iterative way. Ultimately though, I didn't really feel fully satisfied and would inevitably end up tweaking it again.
