[VideoDojo Games](https://videodojo.games/) is an indie game development studio based in the United Kingdom and founded in 2023. I was tasked to design and build the studio's website to help establish its online presence, and provide information about its debut game, *Townbox™*. (Which I'm also working on!)

The website was built using [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com), and is written in good old HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

I designed the site to convey VideoDojo Games' whimsical and playful identity, using a combination of soft colors, bubbly fonts, as well as quirky drawings by my friend [Dom](https://supergroovy.net), who also founded the studio.

This handcrafted vibe also extends to the site's icons, drawn in a purposefully "wonky" style, with unsteady lines that don't quite connect, like they were quickly sketched on a napkin. As one last touch of playfulness, I also integrated an interactive footer (on the desktop version) that has the VideoDojo Games logo follow your cursor!
