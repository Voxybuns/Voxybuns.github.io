In June 2022, I was approached by my friend Dom to assist in the production of their documentary *The Story of PiCPAC*, a deep dive into *Namco*'s lesser known robotics division, and the creation of the eponymous animatronic idol group.

My role was to help define the documentary's art direction and provide graphics for integration in the final edit. I pulled various elements from available promotional material and merchandise to constitute a library of pastel colors, illustrated elements representing the band's members, and patterns that can be assembled into various animated graphics.

I conceived the animated elements and templates in *Adobe After Effects*, using the *Essential Graphics* toolkit for integration in *Adobe Premiere Pro*. Animated patterns are used as backgrounds, and archival photographs are given a screen-printed color treatment, laid out as stills found in a magazine. The presentation is further enhanced by subtle parallax effects and texture overlays that provide additional visual flair and a touch of physicality.
